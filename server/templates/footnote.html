{{define "footnote"}}Profit figures are net of broker fees and sales tax. Broker fees include your Broker Relations skill and your standings toward the Rens station owner and its faction. ISK/day assumes a realistic ~{{fmtCaptureRate}} capture of each item's daily traded volume, not the whole market. Buy and sell prices are the current Heimatar-region order book: the highest buy order anywhere in the region and the lowest sell order anywhere, which may rest at different stations. Volume figures are region-wide too. Orders are placed and filled at Rens, so a region spread is an upper bound and may not be capturable there. Items with thin or manipulated trade history, or a spread resting on a single order, are hidden automatically.{{end}}
