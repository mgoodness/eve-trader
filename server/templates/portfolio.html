Break-even — the list price at which net proceeds cover the position's cost plus estimated sell fees (zero profit). It is a range: the headline high shares the unattributed-fee bucket, the low uses only confidently allocated fees. Target — break-even plus your target net margin, shown as the same range. Mkt net margin — the net margin the current Rens best sell would actually yield, shown only when positive. Status compares the market to break-even (costs covered), not to your target margin. Re-lists are inferred from order snapshots and may undercount.
