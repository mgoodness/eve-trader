# eve-trader v2 spec: Portfolio — market-activity tracking & P/L

Status: **implementation-ready**. Compiled from the decision tickets resolved on the
[wayfinder map "Wayfinder map: v2 transaction & P/L tracking"](https://github.com/mgoodness/eve-trader/issues/85);
each section cites the ticket(s) it derives from. This document is the destination of
that map — no further planning ticket is expected before implementation begins.

v2 builds on v1 (opportunity finder, `docs/spec/v1.md`) and v1.1 (realism/user filters,
net margin, capture-scaled ISK/day). It adds a **Portfolio** view and the ledger behind
it. Aside from the standings fast-follow (§9), v2 does not change the opportunity list.

## 1. Overview

A single-user, hosted web tool. On top of v1.1's Rens opportunity list, v2 tracks the
trading character's own market activity and computes **realized and unrealized P/L per
item**, with re-list broker fees accounted for honestly. It presents a **Portfolio** view
beside the opportunity list.

- **Data:** the character's own ESI wallet, orders, and contracts, kept in a local
  append-only ledger (§3, §5).
- **P/L:** weighted-average cost per item/location, journal-exact cash totals with
  item-estimated fees (§4).
- **Forecast:** per-position break-even/target list prices and market-implied net margin
  (§4.7); per-order relist gain (§4.8). Both are *sequenced after* the core ledger/P&L.
- **Auth:** EVE SSO, expanding the v1 scope set and requiring one re-consent (§6).

**Explicitly out of scope for v2** (see the map's Out of scope section):
- Multi-character / enterprise P/L (ingesting alt wallets). On-behalf-of goods are
  handled as **transfers** (§4.5).
- Detecting items moved via the in-game direct-trade window — ESI exposes no such
  endpoint.
- Corporation wallets, orders, and contracts.
- Backfilling older than ESI's own retention windows.
- Regional arbitrage and trading away from Rens (unchanged from v1).

## 2. Architecture

Unchanged from v1: Go single static binary, embedded SQLite, `html/template` + htmx,
one GCP `e2-micro`, Docker deployment. v2 adds:

- Three ESI data pollers (wallet, orders, contracts) in addition to the existing
  order-book and history pollers (§3, §5.6).
- New authenticated methods on the existing `ESIGateway` seam — wallet
  transactions/journal, open orders, order history, contracts, and contract items —
  implemented by `HTTPGateway` and `Fake` exactly as v1's public methods are.
- A local ledger (§5) and a `/portfolio` route rendering the Portfolio view (§7).
- One re-consent with expanded OAuth scopes (§6).

No new runtime dependencies are introduced by the core P/L work.

## 3. Data sources (ESI)

*(Decided in [ESI wallet, orders, and contracts mechanics for P/L](https://github.com/mgoodness/eve-trader/issues/86),
[C# sample](https://github.com/mgoodness/eve-trader/issues/93), and
[ESI standings and station-owner lookup](https://github.com/mgoodness/eve-trader/issues/97);
primary sources pinned in the research artefacts on the `research/esi-wallet-tracking`
and `research/standings-broker-fees` branches.)*

v2 adds these authenticated routes (v1's public market routes are unchanged):

| Data | Route | Retention | Cache |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wallet transactions | `GET /characters/{id}/wallet/transactions/` | undocumented (assume ~30 d) | 1 h |
| Wallet journal | `GET /characters/{id}/wallet/journal/` | 30 d | 1 h |
| Open orders | `GET /characters/{id}/orders/` | current state | 20 min |
| Order history | `GET /characters/{id}/orders/history/` | 90 d | 1 h |
| Contracts | `GET /characters/{id}/contracts/` | 30 d | 5 min |
| Contract items | `GET /characters/{id}/contracts/{contract_id}/items/` | with contract | 5 min |

Scopes: `esi-wallet.read_character_wallet.v1`, `esi-markets.read_character_orders.v1`,
`esi-contracts.read_character_contracts.v1` (§6). Rate-limit groups: `char-wallet`
150/15 m, `char-contract` 600/15 m — not a constraint for one character.

**Pagination.** Wallet transactions use `from_id` (inclusive — drop the boundary
duplicate); journal, orders, order history, and contracts use `page`/`X-Pages`.

**Linkage (load-bearing).** A transaction joins its journal `market_transaction` entry
via `transaction_id == journal.context_id` where `context_id_type ==
market_transaction_id`. The transactions endpoint's own `journal_ref_id` is
**unreliable** (3/10 matched in the captured sample) and must not be used.
`market_escrow` also carries `context_id` = transaction id; `contract_brokers_fee`
carries `context_id` = contract id.

**Fee/tax attribution is imperfect, by ESI's design.**
- `transaction_tax` and `brokers_fee` journal entries carry **no order or item link**
  (esi/esi-issues#1369, open), so per-item fees cannot be read from the journal.
- Sales tax is deterministic from the known rate (§4.3) and is reconciled against the
  journal total.
- Broker fees are charged **at order placement**, so they may fall outside the 30-day
  journal even when the matching sale is inside it; the journal under-reports them.

**Order-history quirks.**
- It returns only `state ∈ {cancelled, expired}`; **fully-filled orders vanish** from
  both order routes.
- `is_buy_order` (and buy-only fields `escrow`, `min_volume`) are **omitted for sell
  orders** — a missing `is_buy_order` means `false`.
- A re-list is either **cancel-and-recreate** (a new `order_id`, leaving a `cancelled`
  history row) or an **in-place modify** (same `order_id`, `issued` moves). Re-list
  chains are reconstructed heuristically from `(type_id, location_id, side)` ordered by
  `issued`/price; they are inferable, never definitive.

**Transfers.** Item-exchange contracts (`type == "item_exchange"`) plus their items
identify goods that left the character; a zero-price handoff produces no wallet entry
beyond the linked `contract_brokers_fee`. Direct player trades are invisible to ESI.

## 4. P/L model

*(Decided in [On-behalf-of buys](https://github.com/mgoodness/eve-trader/issues/88),
[Define the P/L model](https://github.com/mgoodness/eve-trader/issues/89),
[Re-list-worth-it forecast](https://github.com/mgoodness/eve-trader/issues/91),
[In-place order modifications](https://github.com/mgoodness/eve-trader/issues/95), and
[Relist-gain](https://github.com/mgoodness/eve-trader/issues/96).)*

### 4.1 Cost basis and grain

Weighted-**average cost per (item, location)** — never averaged across stations. Open
positions are derived from the ledger on read (no persisted lots, §5).

### 4.2 Dispositions

A position leaves by exactly one of:
- **Sale** — a market transaction (`market_transaction`).
- **Transfer** — goods that leave the character without a market sale (§4.5).

### 4.3 Fee and tax rates

```
R_b = 3% − 0.3%×BrokerRelations                        // broker-fee rate (§9 adds standings)
R_t = 7.5%×(1 − 0.11×Accounting)                       // sales tax rate
```

- Skill inputs are the character's `active_skill_level` for Broker Relations (`3446`),
  Accounting (`16622`), and **Advanced Broker Relations** (the re-list fee term, §4.4;
  formerly Margin Trading — confirm its type ID from the SDE at implementation).
  Missing = 0.
- **Standings are omitted in v2** (§9 fast-follow), making computed fees a pessimistic
  upper bound.

### 4.4 Fee accounting — journal-exact totals, item-estimated detail

- Actual journal `brokers_fee`/`transaction_tax` entries are the **source of truth** for
  period cash P/L.
- Per-item fees are **estimated** from the formulas below plus best-effort re-list
  attribution; every per-item fee is labelled *estimated*.
- The gap between the summed per-item fees and the journal total is shown as an
  **Unattributed fees** line (mostly re-lists).
- Fees with no position (cancelled/zero-fill orders, and the unfilled remainder of a
  partially filled order) go to an explicit **Sunk fees** bucket — never dropped.

Fee formulas (per order, `max(100 ISK, …)` floor):
- **Placement / cancel-and-recreate:** `max(100 ISK, order_value × R_b)`.
- **In-place modify:** `(1 − (0.50 + 0.06×AdvancedBrokerRelations)) × R_b × NewOrderValue
  + R_b × max(NewOrderValue − OldOrderValue, 0)`, min 100 ISK, where `NewOrderValue =
  new_price × volume_remain` and `OldOrderValue = old_price × volume_remain`.
- A modify we never observed (between polls) cannot be computed; its fee stays
  unattributed.
- An **open, unfilled buy order's** placement fee is **pending**: folded into cost as
  units fill, remainder to sunk on cancel.

Fees enter the basis: buy-side fee → average cost; sell-side fee + sales tax → out of
proceeds.

### 4.5 Transfers

Goods leaving without a market sale are a **transfer**: quantity leaves the position at
average cost, no sale revenue. Detected automatically from item-exchange contracts;
a **manual "mark as transferred"** action (partial-quantity) covers the direct-trade
blind spot. Valued at the contract price when one exists and is > 0, else at cost; the
contract-price-minus-average-cost difference is a separate **transfer gain/loss**, kept
out of trading P/L. Unlinked direct ISK (`player_trading`/`player_donation`) is ignored
for item P/L.

### 4.6 Realized and unrealized P/L

- **Realized P/L** per (item, location): sale proceeds net of sell-side fee and sales
  tax, minus the average cost of units sold (including buy-side fees). Transfer
  gain/loss is reported on its **own line and excluded** from trading Realized P/L.
- **Unrealized P/L**: held/listed quantity valued at the current **Rens best sell net of
  estimated sell fees**; current best buy is available as **liquidation value**. Priced
  at zero where there is no market.

### 4.7 Re-list forecast (sequenced after the P/L foundation)

Per position:
- **Break-even price** — net proceeds cover cost + allocated estimated fees (zero
  profit).
- **Target price** — break-even plus a **target net margin**; a view-level, URL-carried
  control defaulting to **0% net**, so Target = Break-even by default.
- **Market-implied net margin** — the net margin the current Rens best sell would
  actually yield; reported when positive.
- Prices are shown as a **range** (low: confidently-allocated fees; high: sharing the
  unattributed bucket), with the conservative high end as the headline.
- **Status:** `At target now` / `Below target` / `No market`.
- Advisory only: it never places, modifies, or cancels orders.

### 4.8 Relist gain (raise-only)

For each resting sell order priced **below** the current best sell:

```
net = (P_new − P_old) × volume_remain × (1 − R_t) − relistFee
```

where `P_new` is the current best sell (optionally `bestSell − 0.01`), and `relistFee`
is the in-place modify fee (§4.4). "The current best sell" is the best sell among
**other** traders: the character's own resting orders are excluded, since a seller's
own price is always at or below the whole-book best, which would leave nothing to
raise to. Surface only orders with `net > 0`. Buy orders, undercut orders, and orders
already at/above best are excluded.

## 5. Local ledger

*(Decided in [Local ledger](https://github.com/mgoodness/eve-trader/issues/90).)*

**Append-only raw ESI records; positions and P/L computed on read.** All new tables are
created by the existing idempotent `CREATE`/`ALTER` at `db.Open`, backward-compatible
with v1/v1.1 databases.

New tables:
- `wallet_transaction` — keyed by `transaction_id`.
- `wallet_journal` — keyed by journal `id`.
- `character_order` — the character's own orders, **snapshotted by `(order_id, issued)`**
  so in-place modifies and re-list chains survive. Distinct from the public `market_order`.
- `contract` — keyed by `contract_id`.
- `contract_item` — keyed by `(contract_id, record_id)`.
- `manual_transfer` — type, quantity, date, optional counterparty/note/price.
- `ledger_sync` — last-seen transaction id, last journal id/time, and last poll per
  stream, bounding incremental fetch and first-run backfill.

Reused: `item_type` (extended to **every** `type_id` in the ledger, not just the Rens
book), `character_skill` (extended with `advanced_broker_relations_level`),
`esi_token`, `market_order`, `market_history`.

**Polling** (one scheduler, honouring ESI `Expires`): orders ~20 min, wallet
transactions + journal ~1 h, contracts ~15 min.

**Retention:** the local ledger is kept **indefinitely** (no pruning). First run
backfills whatever ESI returns (journal ~30 d, order history ~90 d, contracts ~30 d,
transactions as far as available); downtime longer than a server window loses that slice
permanently.

## 7. Portfolio view

*(Decided in [Portfolio view: what it shows and how](https://github.com/mgoodness/eve-trader/issues/92)
and [Relist-gain](https://github.com/mgoodness/eve-trader/issues/96). Winning prototype on
the [`prototype/portfolio-view-ui` branch](https://github.com/mgoodness/eve-trader/tree/prototype/portfolio-view-ui).)*

A new `/portfolio` route, linked from the header nav beside "Opportunities".

- **A dense position table with action grouping.** One row per position using A's column
  set, grouped by the decision it demands:
  - **At target now** — leads with target price and market-implied net margin.
  - **Below target** — current market → target; break-even shown.
  - **Orders worth moving** — raise-only relist gain (§4.8).
  - **Transfers** — with transfer gain/loss.
  - **No market** — no fabricated price.
  - **Closed / realised** — realized P/L and cumulative estimated fees.
- **Columns:** Item/location, Qty, Avg cost, Mkt sell, Unreal P/L, Realized, Buy/Sell
  re-lists (inferred), Est. fees, Break-even, Target, Mkt net margin, Status.
- **Summary strip:** Realised / Unrealised / Fees (est) / Unattributed fees. (The
  "dashboard" variant is not used.)
- **Footnote + legend:** define Break-even, Target, Market-implied net margin; state that
  per-item fees are estimates (ESI has no fee→order link), that unattributed fees are
  mostly re-lists, and the data's freshness.
- **Exact column order, sort-within-group behaviour, and empty states** are left to the
  implementation; the grouping above is the contract.

## 8. Testing

Following the v1 convention: black-box HTTP tests over the fake `ESIGateway` and a real
ephemeral SQLite database, plus focused package tests.

- **Ledger:** raw-record upsert/idempotency, `(order_id, issued)` snapshots, incremental
  fetch via `ledger_sync`, and the idempotent migration on a v1.1-shaped database.
- **P/L:** seeded transactions/journal/orders/contracts → asserted realized/unrealized
  figures, fee placement (buy into cost, sell out of proceeds), the unattributed and sunk
  buckets, transfer handling (contract and manual), and the `is_buy_order`-missing sell
  case.
- **Forecast / relist-gain:** seeded positions/orders → asserted break-even/target ranges,
  market-implied net margin, statuses, and raise-only gains.
- **Routes:** `/portfolio` renders the groups, columns, summary, and footnote.

## 9. Fast-follows (scoped by this spec, sequenced after v2)

- **Standings in broker-fee calculations**
  ([decision](https://github.com/mgoodness/eve-trader/issues/98); research pinned on
  `research/standings-broker-fees`). Add `esi-characters.read_standings.v1`; resolve the
  station owner via `GET /universe/stations/{id}`, and its faction from a **vendored,
  generated SDE table** (`npcCorporations.jsonl` `factionID` — ESI omits it for NPC
  corps). Apply owner-corp and faction standings **additively** via a single
  `BrokerFee%` used by both the ranking and the portfolio, including the 100 ISK floor
  and the relist formula. Fetch standings daily alongside skills. This removes the
  "pessimistic upper bound" caveat. Open verification: the ESI spec does not state that
  the endpoint returns the **unmodified** standing the fee formula requires.
- **Asset-based reconciliation** — v2 derives positions from the ledger; `/assets` is a
  possible later addition, not a hard exclusion.
