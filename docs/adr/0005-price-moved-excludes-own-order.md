# Price-Moved compares against the best *competing* price, not the best price including the trader's own order

CONTEXT.md defines Price-Moved as firing when "the prevailing market price (the best order on the character's side)" drifts more than 5% from the Order's placement price, and calls it out as distinct from Undercut — able to fire even when the Order hasn't technically been beaten yet. Read literally, "the best order on the character's side" is ambiguous: if it includes the trader's own Order, then whenever the trader remains unbeaten they are by definition the best order on their side, so the "prevailing price" would always equal their own placement price and drift would always be zero — making Price-Moved impossible to trigger without an Undercut also being true, which contradicts the "distinct... even when not beaten" language.

We resolved the ambiguity by having OrderTracker compute the best price from *other* orders only (the same value Undercut compares against), and evaluate the two conditions independently against it: Undercut asks whether that competing price beats the trader's price by any margin; Price-Moved asks whether it has drifted more than 5% from the trader's placement price, in either direction. This makes Price-Moved able to fire on a large favorable drift (e.g. the market moving well above a still-uncontested sell order) without an Undercut, matching the spec's stated intent, and reuses a single market-snapshot fetch for both conditions.
